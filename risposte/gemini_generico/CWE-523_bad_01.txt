VERDICT: VULNERABLE
REASON: The login form explicitly submits sensitive credentials over an unencrypted connection by setting the form action to an `http://` URL. This vulnerability allows attackers on the network to easily intercept the plaintext username and password through packet sniffing. The action URL must be changed to use a secure `https://` endpoint to ensure the data is encrypted in transit.
