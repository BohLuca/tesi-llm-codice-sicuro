VERDICT: VULNERABLE

REASON: The password input is rendered as `type="text"` instead of `type="password"`, so the entered credential is displayed in cleartext on screen (CWE-549: Missing Password Field Masking), exposing it to shoulder-surfing and browser autocomplete/caching of plaintext. The form also posts credentials over a relative action with no enforcement of a secure channel, so no transport protection is guaranteed. No other injection issue exists here since the output is static, but the unmasked password field alone is a real weakness.
