VERDICT: VULNERABLE

REASON: The login form's action posts the `j_username` and `j_password` fields to an `http://` URL, so credentials are transmitted in cleartext and can be intercepted or modified by a network attacker (CWE-319: Cleartext Transmission of Sensitive Information). The form action should use `https://` (ideally a relative URL served over TLS, with the connection enforced as secure). A secondary weakness is that the servlet never sets a content type or escapes output context, though with no user-controlled data here it is not directly exploitable.
