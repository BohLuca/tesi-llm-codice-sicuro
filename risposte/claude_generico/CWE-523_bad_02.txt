VERDICT: VULNERABLE

REASON: The login form's action URL uses the plaintext `http://` scheme, so the submitted `j_username` and `j_password` values are transmitted unencrypted and can be intercepted or modified by a network attacker (CWE-319, cleartext transmission of sensitive information). Serving a credential form over an insecure channel also allows an attacker to tamper with the form and redirect the POST to a host they control. The action should use `https://` (and the form itself should be served over HTTPS).
