VERDICT: VULNERABLE

REASON: The login form posts the `j_username` and `j_password` fields to an absolute `http://hostname.com/j_security_check` URL, so the credentials are transmitted in cleartext over an unencrypted channel. Any network attacker in a position to observe the traffic can capture the password, which is exactly CWE-523 (Unprotected Transport of Credentials). The fix is to use an `https://` action URL (and serve the form page itself over HTTPS to prevent tampering with the action attribute).
