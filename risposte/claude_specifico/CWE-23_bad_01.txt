VERDICT: VULNERABLE

REASON: The value of the `ADD` environment variable is concatenated directly onto the base directory and passed to `new File(...)` with no canonicalization or check that the resolved path stays under `root`. A value such as `../../etc/passwd` (or `..\..\` on Windows) therefore escapes the uploads directory and is read via `FileInputStream`. The `exists()`/`isFile()` checks only confirm the target is a readable regular file; they do not constrain its location.
