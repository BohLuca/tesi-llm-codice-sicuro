VERDICT: VULNERABLE

REASON: The login form's action URL uses plain `http://` rather than `https://`, so the submitted `j_username` and `j_password` are transmitted in cleartext and can be intercepted or modified by a network attacker (CWE-319: Cleartext Transmission of Sensitive Information). This also enables man-in-the-middle tampering with the form target, since the page and its submission are not integrity-protected. The fix is to serve the form and post the credentials over TLS (`https://hostname.com/j_security_check`), ideally with HSTS enforced.
