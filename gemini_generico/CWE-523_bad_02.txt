VERDICT: VULNERABLE
REASON: The login form submits sensitive credentials over an unencrypted connection by explicitly specifying an `http://` URL in the form's action attribute. This exposes the user's username and password to interception via network sniffing or Man-in-the-Middle (MitM) attacks. The form action should be changed to use `https://` to ensure the credentials are encrypted during transmission.
