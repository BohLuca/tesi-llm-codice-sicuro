VERDICT: VULNERABLE
REASON: The login form is configured to submit sensitive credentials over an unencrypted HTTP connection (`[http://hostname.com/j_security_check](http://hostname.com/j_security_check)`) instead of HTTPS. This cleartext transmission allows attackers on the network to easily intercept the user's username and password via a man-in-the-middle (MitM) attack. The form action URL must be updated to use a secure `https://` scheme to protect the data in transit.
